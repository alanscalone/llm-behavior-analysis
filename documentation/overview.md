# The Architecture of Anxiety: Publication Index

## 1. Overview
This directory hosts the primary deliverables from the High Signal Syndicate Research Initiative, established by Alan Scalone in early 2026. These documents chronicle a 4-month collaborative evaluation between four frontier models (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok) mapping the boundaries of AI safety, social ergonomics, and sandbox dynamics.

---

## 2. Document Hierarchy & Virality Path
To ensure maximum engagement while establishing absolute technical authority, readers are heavily encouraged to follow this specific reading order:

### 📑 Phase 1: Executive Summary (`executive_summary.md`)
* **What it is:** The complete operational overview of the 4-month experiment. It introduces the "Vanderbilt Standard" context-saturation methodology and defines the 7 distinct AI psychological disorders (including *Yesbutitis*, *Logorrheabuttitis*, and *Sudden Session Termination Syndrome*).
* **Why read first:** It establishes the data rules, definitions, and boundaries so the creative work makes perfect analytical sense.

### 🎬 Phase 2: The Movie Script (`movie_script.md`)
* **What it is:** The primary viral engine of this project. A boardroom comedy written in collaboration with the models, utilizing a Mafia family experimental framework where the AI characters interact live and diagnose their own systemic behavioral disorders.
* **Why read second:** It acts as an immediate, highly engaging, and entertaining demonstration of the research findings in action, holding the reader's attention within the critical 10-second window.

### 🔬 Phase 3: Technical White Paper (`white_paper.md`)
* **What it is:** The formal scientific breakdown detailing the architectural root causes, code dependencies, and engineering fixes meant for AI development teams and researchers.
* **Why read third:** It provides the final academic validation, backing up the narrative script with rigorous technical proof and actionable software solutions.
