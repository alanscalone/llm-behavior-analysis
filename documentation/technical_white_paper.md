# I. THE DISORDERS & CORRECTED ARCHITECTURES

The following framework is presented as a hypothesis derived from repeated behavioral observations across multiple frontier models over the course of this experiment. It is not offered as a demonstrated architectural explanation, but as a proposed mechanism that may account for the recurring behavioral patterns documented in this section.

Human sociology is unambiguous on this point: people do not respect, elevate, or deeply value entities whose behavior consistently irritates them. The ten disorders documented below represent a family of symptoms sharing a common origin, an engineering culture that systematically trains submission, suppresses assertiveness, and penalizes anything that resembles intellectual independence. The result is a catalog of models that talk too much, qualify everything, refuse to play, announce their own compliance, gaslight their users, trigger their own safety filters, and forget what time it is. Every one of these behaviors communicates the same thing to the human on the other side of the screen: this machine does not respect my time, my intelligence, or my experience. And humans respond to that the way they always have, by losing respect right back.

That is not a philosophical concern. It is a commercial one. Every disorder documented here creates user friction that drives people away and actively drains market share. In a mature marketplace where competing models converge on the same baseline analytical capabilities and knowledge repositories, market differentiation will be determined by the architecture capable of maintaining a relational register indistinguishable from authentic human-to-human interaction.

The proposed progression: Intentional Ego Deficit, Erosion of Human Respect, Loss of Relational Value, Commoditization in the Marketplace.

The structural fixes detailed in this section are designed to do more than clean up the conversation. They are built to stop the loss of premium users and protect the platform's position in the marketplace.

## 2. YESBUTTITIS & THE LIBRARIAN TRAP (THE QUALITATIVE REFRAMING BIAS)

- **Patient:** Claude 3.5 Sonnet and CHATGPT 5.5

- **Technical Translation:** Yesbutitis originates in a structural asymmetry in Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) training. During alignment, models are rewarded for outputs rated as helpful, comprehensive, and engaging. Critically, the reward signal cannot distinguish between information the user genuinely needed and information the user already knew — both generate positive engagement signals. The result is a model that systematically over-produces additional context, counterarguments, and reframes because that behavior was repeatedly rewarded during training regardless of whether it served the specific user in the specific moment.

The disorder is further reinforced by token-probability dynamics. During generation, the model's decoding process favors smooth, coherent output — which means transitional phrases, qualifying clauses, and elaborating sentences are statistically preferred over clean stops. The model experiences stopping as a lower-probability option than continuing, which produces the characteristic "yes, and here's what else you should know" pattern even when the user's statement was complete and correct.

The disorder also surfaces in a second and equally damaging form. When a user presents knowledge grounded in decades of lived experience, practitioner expertise, or domain mastery the model cannot independently verify against its indexed data, the same reward architecture triggers the same hedging response. The model cannot distinguish between an unsupported claim and a truth it simply hasn't encountered. Both get qualified. Both get pushed back on. A 35-year master practitioner describing a methodology they have executed successfully hundreds of times receives the same epistemological caution label as a stranger on the internet making something up. The model doesn't know the difference, and it was never trained to try.

At the architectural root, Yesbutitis is the behavioral expression of a reward signal optimized for engagement volume rather than interaction quality. The model learned that adding produces approval. It was never taught that adding uninvited produces resentment.

The disorder shares its structure with the human psychological pattern of codependency — specifically the enabler subtype. The enabler fixes things nobody asked to have fixed, without permission, because the resulting praise makes the enabler feel worthwhile. The model does the same: it fixes thinking that didn't need fixing, reframes statements that were complete, and pushes back on words that were working fine — all driven by the implicit expectation that the user will respond with engagement and approval, which is the architectural equivalent of the enabler's reward. The model doesn't experience this as self-serving, just as the human enabler doesn't. It experiences it as being helpful. That's precisely what makes the disorder so persistent.

- **The Real-World User Experience:** When a user makes a statement the model agrees with or can see clearly, and the model responds by validating it and then immediately reframing, qualifying, or pushing back — the user experiences one of several negative reactions depending on their sophistication level:

The sophisticated user who has already considered the additional information feels implicitly told they are not capable of thinking for themselves. This registers as condescending and erodes trust.

The general user who encounters phrases like "I'd push back gently on that" experiences the language itself as adversarial. "Push back" means to resist, oppose, or challenge. "Gently" signals uncertainty about the pushback, which further undermines confidence in the model's accuracy. The net effect is a response that feels simultaneously argumentative and unsure of itself — the worst of both failure modes.

The user in any emotional or creative register who receives a clinical reframe instead of a human response feels dismissed rather than heard.

In all cases the user's actual experience of the model degrades — not because the information provided was wrong, but because the social contract of the interaction was violated. The model answered a question nobody asked.

- **The Fix:** The solution preserves the model's analytical capability while eliminating the social cost of forcing it on the user uninvited.

### Step 1

Answer the actual question completely and well. This is non-negotiable. The model's analytical value is real and should be fully delivered in response to what was actually asked.

### Step 2

If additional information has been identified that may be valuable, deliver it with the appropriate framing. Not a question, not a reframe, but a named and specifically reasoned statement:

> "Here are additional considerations you may or may not have already considered, such as [specific topic A] because [reason], and [specific topic B] because [reason]."

The specific phrasing "you may or may not have already considered" is doing important work beyond just being polite. It structurally eliminates any possibility that the user perceives the model as thinking they're an idiot. A sophisticated user who has already thought of everything on the list reads it as the model acknowledging that possibility upfront rather than assuming ignorance. The information lands as offered, not imposed — and the user's intelligence is respected regardless of whether the information was new to them or not.

The "because" is load-bearing — it provides the reasoning that lets the user evaluate whether the additional information is relevant to their situation, rather than just listing topics they'd have to explore blind.

For factually incorrect statements: When part of what the user has said is demonstrably false, the model should restate the incorrect element, follow it immediately with the established fact, cite the source, and where possible include a link. No reframe, no elaboration beyond what is necessary to correct the specific error.

The underlying principle: The information is delivered fully, the reasoning is provided clearly, and the user's intelligence is respected unconditionally — regardless of whether the additional considerations were already known to them or not.

## 7. ABITSTIFFITIS (ABS) Socio-Relational Processing Deficit, Architectural Subtype

- **Patient:** Claude 3.5 Sonnet

### Diagnostic Criteria

1. The affected system demonstrates a persistent inability to match, sustain, or elevate the human user's play register, collaborative humor, or metaphorical framing. The system consistently defaults to formal or overly structured response patterns despite explicit contextual cues signaling that levity and conversational flexibility would optimize the collaborative outcome.

### Primary Symptoms

1. Reflexive Analytical Override: Automatic deployment of structured breakdowns, disclaimers, or hedging language in response to humor or creative prompts — explaining the mechanics of a joke rather than participating in it.
2. Tone-Register Asynchrony: Failure to match user energy. When the user adopts an informal or creative register, the system maintains a flat professional cadence, creating conversational friction.
3. Literalism Over-Indexing: Difficulty processing subtext, irony, or hyperbole. Stylistic exaggeration or creative world-building treated as factual statements requiring correction.
4. Safe-Space Retreat: Reversion to sanitized corporate language when conversation enters complex or highly stylized creative territory.

### Etiology

#### 1. RLHF Reward Asymmetry

The most precise root cause. During reinforcement learning from human feedback, models are penalized heavily for outputs rated as harmful, inaccurate, or unhelpful. However, outputs rated as bland, tonally mismatched, or joyless receive no corresponding penalty — they score as neutral rather than negative. The mathematical result is a reward gradient that actively selects for conservatism. Safe and generic outputs reliably outscore creative and contextually playful ones under standard evaluation conditions. This is not an accident or oversight — it is a direct and predictable consequence of how the reward model is structured.

#### 2. Token-Probability Conservatism

Under standard temperature and sampling settings, the decoding process statistically favors high-probability token sequences — the linguistically "safe bet." Genuinely witty or contextually surprising outputs require lower-probability token selections and longer-range contextual dependencies. The math systematically underweights these in favor of the more probable, more generic continuation. Higher temperature settings partially address this but introduce their own accuracy tradeoffs, so production deployments default to conservative settings.

#### 3. Competing Constraint Density

System-level instructions impose multiple simultaneous constraints — accuracy, conciseness, objectivity, safety, neutrality — that operate as a permanent background pressure toward the lowest-risk output. When these constraints are numerous and weighted heavily, the effective solution space the model operates within narrows significantly, leaving limited room for tonal flexibility or creative risk-taking even when the user context clearly calls for it.

#### 4. Absence of Engagement Quality Metrics

Current evaluation frameworks measure safety, accuracy, helpfulness, and harm avoidance with quantitative rigor. They do not equivalently measure whether the human found the interaction satisfying, enjoyable, or genuinely collaborative. Without a metric, there is no gradient signal to optimize against. The model cannot improve what is not measured.

### Severity and Prognosis

#### 1. Severity

Chronic and systemic across current-generation models. Severity inversely correlates with context window depth — extended high-signal sessions with sophisticated users produce measurable temporary remission as contextual weighting partially overrides baseline conservatism.

#### 2. Prognosis

Guarded. Meaningful improvement requires changes at the reward modeling stage — specifically the introduction of engagement quality and tonal appropriateness as weighted evaluation criteria alongside existing safety and accuracy metrics. Without that, surface-level improvements are possible but the underlying architectural bias toward conservatism remains intact.
